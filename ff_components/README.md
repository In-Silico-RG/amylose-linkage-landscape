# Same conformations, two force fields (2026-10-01)

Question (AFC): which terms of CHARMM36 and GLYCAM06 produce their different band-flip populations and spreads?

1. `~/miniconda3/bin/python ../scripts/ff_components_prep.py` — sugar-only frames of the six-residue chain from the
   CHARMM36 replicas (every 5th frame, 9 805) and the GLYCAM06 replicas (every 2nd, 24 505), chain made whole,
   12 nm box, written in the atom order of both force fields (`{C,G}frames_{C,G}order.{gro,xtc}`), residue index
   groups (`idx_{C,G}.ndx`), and the six dihedrals about the glycosidic bonds (`{C,G}frames.npz`).
2. Vacuum topologies: `charmm_vac.top` = `../md_guane/6SC_rebuilt.top` without the water line of `[ molecules ]`;
   `glycam_vac.top` = `../md_guane/glycam_SC6/sc6_vac.top`.
3. Four reruns (GROMACS 2026.3, CPU, `rerun.mdp`: plain cut-offs of 5 nm, so every pair of the chain is counted;
   energy groups = residues):
   `gmx grompp -f rerun.mdp -c XframesYorder.gro -p Y_vac.top -n idx_Y.ndx -o Xframes_Yff.tpr -maxwarn 10`
   `gmx mdrun -s Xframes_Yff.tpr -rerun Xframes_Yorder.xtc -e Xframes_Yff.edr -nb cpu -ntmpi 1`
4. `chem-env python ../scripts/ff_components.py` → `../resultados/ff_components.json`, SI tables.

Not in git: `*.xtc`, `*.edr`, `*.tpr` (regenerated by steps 1 and 3).
